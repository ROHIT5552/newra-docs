# NewRa Lens Energy Intelligence

**NewRa Lens** is the automated electricity bill analysis and tariff extraction engine of the NewRa platform.

---

## 🔍 Core Features

1. **Automated Bill Parsing**:
   * Scans monthly utility DISCOM bills (PDF, scans, electronic receipts).
   * Extracts contracted maximum demand (CMD), sanctioned load, time-of-day (TOD) consumption tariffs, fuel adjustment charges (FAC), and historical power factor penalties.
2. **Savings & Substitution Modeling**:
   * Computes the exact green energy replacement percentage (e.g., 25%, 50%, or 80% solar substitution).
   * Generates audit-ready financial comparisons delivered to the GES workspace.
