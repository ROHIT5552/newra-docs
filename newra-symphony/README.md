# NewRa Symphony & Analytics

**NewRa Symphony** manages energy delivery reconciliation, operational data pipelines, and developer work tracking across generation portfolios.

---

## ⚡ Key Responsibilities

1. **Monthly Settlement Reconciliation**:
   * Matches generation schedules with actual meter data recorded at grid sub-stations.
   * Tracks deviations, banking adjustments, and green energy credits.
2. **Work Tracker & Progress Logging**:
   * Logs milestone completions across engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) teams.
   * Feeds verified technical reports directly into the Core CRM Go-Live stage gates.
