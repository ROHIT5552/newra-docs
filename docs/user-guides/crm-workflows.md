# CRM & Pipeline Workflows

The **NewRa Core CRM** manages renewable energy opportunities from initial lead capture through multi-year commercial operation.

---

## 📝 Key Functional Panels

### 1. Account Detail & Verification
* View basic corporate details (Legal Name, GSTIN, CIN, Registered Office).
* Access associated contact persons and designated account managers.
* Track historical stage advancement timestamps and reviewer notes.

### 2. Supply Allocation Panel
* **Capacity Proposing**: Propose capacity (in MW) from verified GEP solar or hybrid parks to specific GES consumers.
* **Contracting & Tenure**: Specify contract duration (e.g. 10 to 25 years), delivery point, and tariff terms.
* **Readiness Verification**: Record DISCOM metering references, CEIG approvals, and delivery checkpoints before activating supply.

### 3. Deal Desk & Financial Simulator
* Compare existing grid tariffs vs. open-access renewable power tariffs.
* Factor in cross-subsidy surcharges (CSS), additional surcharges (AS), and wheeling charges.
* Model projected 5-year, 10-year, and 15-year cumulative cost savings.

### 4. Integrated Document Vault & eSign
* Securely upload and preview PDF evidence (electricity bills, land documents, grid connectivity letters).
* Initiate multi-party digital signatures with full audit trail logging.
