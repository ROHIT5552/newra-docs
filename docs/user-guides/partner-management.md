# Partner & Generator Management

The **Green Energy Producer (GEP)** portal allows renewable park developers and IPPs to list generation capacity, track statutory milestones, and contract power.

---

## 🏗️ Developer Milestone Tracking

1. **Project Registration**:
   * Plant technology (Ground-mounted Solar, Wind, Wind-Solar Hybrid).
   * Location, pooling substation, and evacuation voltage (33kV / 66kV / 132kV).
2. **Statutory & Environmental Clearances**:
   * Land title and conversion documents.
   * State Nodal Agency (SNA) registration and CEIG safety inspection clearance.
3. **Connectivity & Evacuation Approval**:
   * Grid connectivity feasibility letter from State Transmission Utility (STU).
4. **Capacity Allocation**:
   * Linking plant generation blocks to creditworthy C&I subscribers via NewRa MarketMatch.
