# Subscriber Onboarding & Energy Transition

The **Green Energy Subscriber (GES)** workflow governs the process for commercial and industrial consumers procuring off-site green power.

---

## 🛤️ Subscriber Journey Steps

1. **Electricity Bill Submission**:
   * The subscriber uploads recent DISCOM electricity bills.
   * NewRa Lens automatically processes billing demand, contract load, and time-of-day tariff rates.
2. **Savings & Technical Feasibility**:
   * NewRa models the maximum allowable renewable power substitution under state open-access regulations.
   * Generates a comparative financial proposal highlighting annual operational savings.
3. **LOI & Legal Execution**:
   * Letter of Intent (LOI) is issued and accepted.
   * Execution of standard NDA and multi-year Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) via integrated eSign.
4. **Open Access Permitting & Synchronization**:
   * State Load Despatch Centre (SLDC) and DISCOM open access applications are submitted.
   * ABT/TOD smart metering installation is verified.
5. **Commercial Operation Date (COD)**:
   * First green energy scheduling begins with daily and monthly energy reconciliation.
