# NewRa Atlas Geospatial Infrastructure

**NewRa Atlas** is the geospatial information system (GIS) mapping solar plants, wind turbines, substations, and transmission networks.

---

## 🗺️ Asset Mapping & Connectivity

1. **Grid Node Routing**:
   * Visualizes transmission evacuation lines from generation assets to State Transmission Utilities (STU) and Central Transmission Utilities (CTU).
2. **Substation Capacity Tracking**:
   * Maps available injection capacity across local 33kV, 66kV, and 132kV pooling stations to optimize future plant siting.
